I firmly believe you need the right mindset going into this expansion in order to fully enjoy it. The story is split in two halves, one where the Warrior of Light accompanies Wuk Lamat in her quest to become the next Dawnservant and the second, where the whole cast faces a sudden otherworldy threat to Tural. Both sections are interesting and just as rewarding for paying attention, but its no surprise that everyone enjoys the latter half more. The start is a little slow but theres still plenty of fun to have with it, a lot of knowledge you gain of Tural's culture really enhances the overall experience too. I loved that story halves feel so different, the first half teaches us so much about Tural from its culture to its many different people, while the second half serves as a fantastical dilemma to solve. 

The characters are all well written and explored through out the expansion, it was great to watch Wuk Lamat grow during her journey for the keystones and see exactly what she learns during it. I loved her realization that she alone is not fit for Dawnservant, and rather ruling with the help of her brother would be the best outcome. Erenville and Krile both get some interesting subplots during the expansion that expand on them as characters in their entirety, something that both characters really benefitted from. 

The area designs are phenomenal across the entire expansion, each one being different from the last but reasonably coexisting with one another. The design of the final area is gorgeous and was well worth the hype. The battle content is probably some of the best out of the game so far, lots of unique dungeons and bosses here. Per usual, the difficulty also spikes quite a bit which was loads of fun. The extra challenge made for a refreshing experience, I want the entire party to wipe on new content if you can believe it! The music was also amazing across the board, not that this game's music ever disappoints. When I consider that this is likely the "A Realm Reborn" of the new story arc its blows my mind, Square Enix did great starting the story a new, a task that was surely daunting. Like I said in the beginning, the expansion isn't any Endwalker, so don't expect that and you'll likely find it to be thoroughly enjoyable.
