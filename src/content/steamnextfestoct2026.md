# There are No Ghosts at the Grand
<figure>
  <img
    src="/images/embedded/noghostsatthegrand.jpg"
    alt="Image of gameplay for There are No Ghosts at the Grand."
  />
</figure>

*There are No Ghosts at the Grand* is the debut game for Friday Sundae, an indie team working out of Bristol, England. On the surface, *There are No Ghosts at the Grand* seems like your typical restoration simulator, packing your character with multiple tools to renovate a hotel. Join Chris David as he attempts to fix up a dilapidated English hotel that he has suddenly inherited from his late grandfather, easy as pie… right? But the Grand and the town around it harbor an interesting twist filled with mystery, the supernatural, and music!

While I only played the first demo out of three available, I was pleasantly surprised by the full sampling of gameplay they give the player. Starting with renovating the lounge of the Grand, you almost think you know what to expect for the game. Except over the next 30 minutes you drive and crash your boat, get stranded on a remote island, decorate a WWII bunker, and fight weird chair spiders. Even from the small glimpse into the demo, it is clear that *There are No Ghosts at the Grand* manages to excel in multiple areas to create a game that is truly one of a kind, full of witty humor, unique music, and polished gameplay.

Play the full release of *There are No Ghosts at the Grand* in December this year and wishlist [here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3992630/There_Are_No_Ghosts_at_the_Grand_Demo/?curator_clanid=39049601 "Steam Page")!

# Probably Stolen
<figure>
  <img
    src="/images/embedded/probablystolen.jpg"
    alt="Image of gameplay for Probably Stolen."
  />
</figure>

*Probably Stolen* is a new upcoming store management from indie studio Questing Goose Studios in Montreal, Canada. Keep and manage a shop in a dystopian cyberpunk world by buying, trading, and selling various items such as, food, materials, household goods, and more! But things won’t be so easy as you keep an eye out for wanted criminals and counterfeit items in a style inspired by Papers, Please. *Probably Stolen* merges management, crafting, and upgrade elements to create a unique experience with so many different features and ideas present.

Upon first impression, *Probably Stolen* seems like a simple game, but it isn’t long before they throw so many different elements at you to track while still trying to turn a profit. I know for a fact that my nearly 45 minutes in the demo isn’t nearly enough to see all that this game has in store so far. On start the player is able to select something to specialize in, unlocking various specializations such as crafting weapons, creating wine, and housing animals. There is no doubt that this game has hours of content to explore within just the free demo. If you are itching to manage inventories, keep an eye out for criminals, and manage your own store, then *Probably Stolen* is easily worth a shot to tickle the side of your brain that loved Papers, Please! Yet, be prepared to manage a daunting amount of menus and inventories to create a shop that is able to stay open and please your clientele. 

Play the full release of *Probably Stolen* on October 28th this year and wishlist [here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4349200/Probably_Stolen_Demo/?curator_clanid=39049601 "Steam Page")!

# Demi and the Fractured Dream
<figure>
  <img
    src="/images/embedded/demiandthefractureddream.jpg"
    alt="Image of gameplay for Demi and the Fractured Dream."
  />
</figure>

*Demi and the Fractured Dream* is an action adventure game with a beautiful style coming out of Yarn Owl based in Austin, Texas. Voidsent Demi awakens in an abandoned temple and is instantly thrust into action, fighting enemies and gaining his quest to defeat the Accursed Beasts that threaten this world, Somnus. With a classic action-adventure combat system there are many ways to change Demi’s playstyle to fit one you love best. But along the way meet the various unique denizens of Somnus and explore the areas they inhabit, solving puzzles, and finding treasure. *Demi and the Fractured Dream* comes as a breath of fresh air in its genre and calls to any who enjoy The Legend of Zelda series while still being wholeheartedly original in its presentation.

A demo that I genuinely didn’t want to end. From the very start I found myself immersed by the environments I was in and deeply interested in all that the combat system had to offer this early on. *Demi and the Fractured Dream’s* demo gives a perfect sampling of the beautiful environments, unique characters, and rewarding combat of the full game. After just 40 minutes, I was desperate to play more and devastated to see the thank you screen. I have no doubt that if *Demi and the Fractured Dream* can reach the right audience, it will find its way as an influential indie game.

Play the full release of Demi and the Fractured Dream in February of next year and wishlist [here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1725710/Demi_and_the_Fractured_Dream/ "Steam Page")!

# Apple Crumble
<figure>
  <img
    src="/images/embedded/applecrumble.png"
    alt="Image of gameplay for Apple Crumble."
  />
</figure>

*Apple Crumble* is a brand new story game from the Berlin developers that brought you Duck Detective. Joining your bizarre and dysfunctional family to celebrate your Grandma’s birthday, but something seems wrong. Can Jess truly trust her family or their motives? And why are they acting so strange? And who the hell is the random guy in your bedroom? *Apple Crumble* merges coziness and mystery to concoct a perfect little game to snuggle up with if you enjoy something akin to What Remains of Edith Finch or Life is Strange.

The demo roughly covers nearly a third of the game, but is very impressive from the start. The characters in Jess’ family are exactly what you would expect to see represented in a dysfunctional family; the overbearing mother, the “too cool for you” older sister, the spoiled younger sister, and a zany uncle. But it is clear there is more to this little family getting together as characters exchange hushed whispers, brush off important events, and act unfairly to one another. Leaving off on a cliff hanger, *Apple Crumble* keeps you begging for more and desiring to see this story to its end.

Play the full release of *Apple Crumble* on December 3rd this year and wishlist [here](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4110770/Apple_Crumble/?curator_clanid=39049601 "Steam Page")!
