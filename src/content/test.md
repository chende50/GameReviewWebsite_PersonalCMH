Final Fantasy XIV's Stormblood was quite an enjoyable expansion, only slightly weak because it falls after Heavensward which I enjoyed substantially more. The plot is your typical rebellion story but has a unique twist as the player's character, the Warrior of Light, liberates two nations, Ala Mhigo & Doma. I found the latter half of the story in Doma more interesting than the first half, truthfully the time the crew spends in Doma roped me in more than the Ala Mhigan side of things ever did. Doma was full of loveable supporting characters and new locations that felt more enjoyable to explore. On the other hand Ala Mhigo was much more lackluster, especially the new areas. The dungeons/trials in this expansion were great, every time I unlocked a new one I was ecstatic to queue for it (something that I never felt in ARR and only occasionally felt in HW). The music for this expansion is also good, especially the boss music that plays in the dungeons!

Unfortunately this expansion has its drawbacks. The biggest supporting character, Lyse, is written rather poorly. Her character would be more fun if she truly was a new character as the reveal that she was acting as Yda for two expansions makes her character flounder. She is written originally as naive and reckless but with time gains a sense of responsibility and leadership, something she should already know from acting as a Scion, but if they were to remove her orgin of acting as Yda she would lose an incredible amount of depth. I wanted so badly to like her but found it hard until probably the final cutscenes. A few minor notes and compliants, the dialouge writing suffers occasionally and sounds goofy or took me out of the story. A few moments with voice actors had this same effect (often with Lyse and Zenos). Lastly, the final set of cutscenes perfectly both tie this expansion together yet keeps me starving for more, plenty of loose ends are tied and cliffhangers or major reveals are shown that made me beyond intrigued.

Editor's Note: Since writing this review, I've adjusted my rating for Stormblood. Simply put, Stormblood doesn't hold up well as I played the rest of the expansions in this game. At some point, I felt that my rating of Stormblood was too high in retrospect of the entire game. This might also be why the review seems to have higher praise than the rating the expansion recieved.
