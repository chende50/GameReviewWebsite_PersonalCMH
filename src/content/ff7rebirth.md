This game is a beautiful remake and reimagining of my favorite portions of the original FFVII. The purists probably hate it because its not the exact remake they might have wanted but this game might be the best game I have ever played. The story in this game feels very refreshing, despite a substantial amount of it being the same as the original. I was constantly filled with wonder and excitement to play even though I "technically" knew everything that would happen, a feat that is rather impressive when creating a remake. Rebirth introduces many new characters while building on the returning ones and truthfully, the devs blew it out of the water with the character and party interactions. Each character not only feels important to Cloud but also other party members, such as Red XIII and Aerith having a close friendship, Aerith and Tifa giggling and teasing Cloud together, Tifa and Yuffie acting like siblings, and Barrett and Yuffie having an adorable bond built around their large age difference. It makes the story so much more impactful and sometimes gutwrenching as you come to adore these characters and realize that they adore one another too. Especially as the main character, Cloud's characterization in this game is phenomenal. As the game progresses you get the joy of seeing his personality shift as he loses his identity, creating haunting moments that will rock you to the core. I could ramble forever about the characters in this game, as I find that each one of them holds a deep place in my heart after all this time.

The open world is extremely expansive and can be rather rewarding, though occasionally overwhelming. It was hard to balance story and exploration but there is a balance that exists for every player. Upon finding that balance, I found the exploration very enjoyable and felt it eventually balanced out well for me. Regardless, I ended with about 84 hours on the game and still have a lot of content to explore so it's not surprising the game can feel overwhelming with all there is to do. Alongside this open world content are a multitude of sidequests across a vast amount of areas. The sidequests were really fun for the most part, I didn't love all of them but there were plenty that made me laugh, smile, and sometimes tear up. They help support a new mechanic that allows the player to track their bond with each party member, something that I was glad was no longer a hidden value.

The gameplay speaks for itself in so many ways, bolstering an already well made game into something genuinely fun to play. The combat is a lot like remake's but it only gets better as each character unlocks new abilities and can be played in many different ways thus having the potential to make characters very strong! With a diverse cast of characters, there are so many different playstyles that Rebirth can cater to. I personally felt the most rewarded by Aerith and Yuffie's combat, as both contain unique components that make the very entertaining to play.

And Holy shit, the music in this game is insane. So many amazing tracks for the original game are remade in such an honorable way that completely rounds out Rebirth as a whole. Yet they don't stop there, Rebirth has so many original music that amplifies its gameplay and scenes more than you can imagine. Personally, I'm still humming the Queen's Blood theme.

Overall this game is a gem among modern day games and the devs did a perfect job both remaking and reimagining all of the segments of the game covered. If it tells you anything about how much I cared, I cried about 16 times while playing through this game. Many of these being at the endings as the devs created such a beatiful, heartwrenching, unique yet familiar ending to this installment of the trilogy. I found myself reaching moments that I had looked forward to seeing in the game and whispering to myself "No no no I don't want to do it" I was terrified and I loved it, exactly how I felt playing the original a few years back. There are things in this game that I could nitpick if I wanted to but it doesn't feel worth it because the overarching final impression is that it is truly perfection in my eyes. While I wasn't happy with everything it was all so small that it wasn'tworth trying to remember and mention. I'm absolutely bias about this game but I think I just witnessed perfection. 

Editors Note: Now that my intial recency bias has most definitely subsided, I still love this game. But the story isn't completely without its flaws and I do question if all the changes in story will pay off in the 3rd installment. The reality is that part three could totally ruin this trilogy for me and other players if they don't manage to wrap up their loose ends. I enjoy what they did with the whispers in this game, the battle between Aerith and Sephiroth's desired fates/whispers on top of the regular whispers makes the whole whisper subplot far more interesting. The timeline shenanigans could totally pay off in the future but they can also totally drop the ball too. I am eagerly awaiting to see where it takes us. On the other hand, I am still unsure of the role they have Zack playing in this game and hope the story with him has more of a pay off than his appearance during the final battles in Rebirth. I'm nervous and maybe a little skeptical about part three but still have faith in all they have planned.
