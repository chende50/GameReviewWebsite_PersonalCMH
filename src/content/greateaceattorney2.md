> *"May you feel the jaws of the beast at your throat every time you swallow"*

What a phenomenal sequel to the first game in this duology. I'd argue this one blows it out of the water in comparison, containing so many more interesting story beats, mysteries, and characters. The first case, The Adventure of the Blossoming Attorney, is outstanding for a tutorial case and so unique as we see Susato, a mere judicial assistant, briefly act as a defense lawyer in Japan to save a close friend of hers. The case is nice because its familiar, serves as a good refresher for mechanics, and is still relevant to the later plot of the game. Our second case, The Memoirs of the Clouded Kokoro, is mostly the typical filler case at this point in theses games but still has a nice twist, revealing it actually took place within the first game. The characters are fun and it has plenty of good moments while still propelling the overarching story. After the court hearing resolves, we are given a major plot point to help build anticipation, the discovery of the Baskerville collar. This discovery deepened my intrigue but also left me with a long list of questions to ponder about the remainder of the game. I think its discovery in chapter two serves well for the whole game, keeping things from becoming stale. The third case, The Return of the Great Departed Soul, was slightly underwhelming initially but does begin to pick up, slowly intertwining with the mysterious Professor cases we are beginning to learn about. Despite this, I can't say I found this case to be memorable but it serves its point well withing the scope of the overall game. 

The final two cases, Twisted Karma and His Last Bow & The Resolve of Ryunosuke Naruhodo, are practically one entire chapter so I want to give my opinions on them together. In a classic Ace Attorney style, the finales are always outstanding and this game does not falter from that. The fourth chapter is a nice set up for whats to come and yet still remains interesting in its own regards, showing Ryunosuke chip his way ever closer to the heart of this game's story. But it all truly comes out in the official final chapter, which was full of endless twists and astonishing moments. I found what I liked most is that each of these twists and revelations were inherently predictable thanks to the aggressive foreshadowing in this game and the installment before that. I say this as a positive note, the foreshadowing is there yet I did not predict the entire ending. Rather, I felt rewarded for paying attention enough to be able to sleuth out some of the twist before it was revealed. If you've paid attention to the game you will likely be able to predict parts of the overarching story and ending, while still enjoying the ride it take to get there. To me that means the developers wrote a great game! The music is just as great in this game, if not better than the first. For the mechanics, they remain the same from the first so nothing to say there. I greatly enjoyed the characters in this game, both new and old, especially seeing their development over time between both bad and good.
